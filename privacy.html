Privacy · Version 1.0

Your game stays yours.

Lantern Relay does not collect, transmit, sell, or track personal data. This policy describes the iPhone app and the separate support links it opens.

App data: The app works offline without an account, advertising, analytics, or third-party SDKs. Game data stays on the device unless it is included in a backup managed by Apple under the device owner’s settings.

Data stored on your iPhone

Lantern Relay saves campaign progress, the active game, and your gentle haptics preference in Apple’s local preferences storage (UserDefaults). The app reads this data to restore your game and settings. It is not transmitted to the developer or another service by the app.

Apple may include app data in an iPhone or iCloud device backup according to your device settings. Any such backup is managed by Apple under Apple’s privacy terms. The developer cannot access it. Reset all progress in Field guide to erase campaign results and the active game from the device; this does not change the haptics preference or remove copies that may already exist in a device backup. Deleting the app removes its local app data from the device, subject to any separate backup.

Collection, tracking, and sharing

The app collects no personal data and sends no data over the network. It has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs. The app does not share data with the developer or other third parties.

Website and support links

The Privacy and Help & support links open pages hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and may process other website request information under its General Privacy Statement. The Lantern Relay pages contain no embedded analytics or advertising.

The support link opens GitHub Issues. Issues and comments submitted there are handled by GitHub and may be publicly visible. The developer can read the information you choose to include to respond to your request and improve the app. Do not include passwords, financial details, health information, or other sensitive personal information in a public issue. See GitHub’s General Privacy Statement.

Children

Lantern Relay does not ask for or collect personal information from children or other users. The App Store age rating will be set from the final content and Apple’s age-rating questionnaire.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe the change. The date below identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For a privacy question, open a GitHub support issue. Posts are public, so avoid including personal information. You can also review GitHub’s privacy statement for information it processes on its own services.
